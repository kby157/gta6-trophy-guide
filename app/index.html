    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no difficulty setting and no mission replay, so save manually at the start of each chapter and keep cheats off (they disable trophies and saving). Published roadmaps put the platinum at roughly 200 hours and call A Long Road, the 100% trophy, the hardest on the list. Sources: GTABase Trophy Guide; PowerPyx roadmap; Push Square Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

    Missable & watch trophies

    Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable by the published guides: Positive Mental Attitude. Five stranger finales carry a WATCH flag instead: one source reports their chains have windows tied to story progress, another says the quest givers return after the story. Both versions are shown on each of those trophies; the safe play is to start the chains when they are offered.
