    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no difficulty setting and no mission replay, so save manually at the start of each chapter and keep cheats off (they disable trophies and saving). Published roadmaps put the platinum at roughly 200 hours and call A Long Road, the 100% trophy, the hardest on the list. Sources: GTABase Trophy Guide; PowerPyx roadmap; Push Square Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

    Missable & watch trophies

    Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Five stranger finales carry a WATCH flag: one source reports windows on their chains, another says the quest givers return after the story. Both versions are shown on each trophy; start chains when offered.
