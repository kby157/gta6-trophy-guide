    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no difficulty setting, no mission replay: save manually at each chapter start; keep cheats off (they disable trophies and saving). Roadmaps put the platinum at roughly 200 hours and call A Long Road (100%) the hardest trophy. Sources: GTABase Trophy Guide; PowerPyx roadmap; Push Square Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

    Missable & watch trophies

    Only one trophy is reported possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Five stranger finales carry a WATCH flag — one source reports windows on their chains, another says the quest givers return. Both versions are on each trophy; start chains when offered.
